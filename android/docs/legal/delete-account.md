# Delete your VeilWalker account

**In the app (fastest):** open VeilWalker → *@ account* → *Delete account* → type `DELETE`.

This immediately:
- deletes your synced data from our server (daily step totals and any encrypted backup), and
- wipes all VeilWalker data from your phone.

**Kept:** your handle stays reserved so nobody can impersonate it. Signed point records may be kept, linked only to a random public key that's no longer associated with you, so the ledger stays verifiable.

**Can't open the app?** Email premiselayer@gmail.com with your handle. Because there's no email or password on file, we'll ask you to prove ownership by signing a short message with your recovery phrase (the app can do this when reinstalled and restored). We complete deletions within 30 days.
