# VeilWalker Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

*Effective: October 28, 2026 · DRAFT pending legal review*

This policy is written for the Washington My Health My Data Act and similar state consumer health data laws (including Nevada and Connecticut). It supplements our [Privacy Policy](privacy).

## Consumer health data we collect

- **Step counts** from your phone's step sensor
- **Motion-sensor data** (accelerometer, gyroscope) during walks
- **Location data** (GPS) during walks
- **Physical activity** detected by Android (walking or not)
- Derived data: daily step totals, walk duration and distance, anti-cheat scores

## How we collect it

Directly from your phone's sensors, only after you give separate, affirmative consent on first launch. Nothing is collected before that.

## Why we use it

To count steps, run walks, award points and badges, progress virtual routes, and detect fake activity. We don't use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

## Where it goes

- **Stays on your device:** raw sensor data and GPS traces (deleted after 30 days).
- **Sent to us:** daily step totals by type and an anti-cheat score, signed by your device. Sent over Tor by default.
- **Shared with third parties:** none. Our hosting provider (Railway) stores our database on our behalf as a processor and can't use it for anything else.
- **Sold:** never.

## Your rights

- **Withdraw consent and delete** at any time: *@ account → Delete account* in the app.
- **Confirm and access** what we hold, or **appeal** a decision about your request, by contacting us below. We respond within 45 days.
