# Play Store listing — VeilWalker

**App name (30):** VeilWalker: Private Step Walks

**Short description (80):**
A private step counter: walk real history, collect badges, Tor on by default.

**Full description (≤4000):**

VeilWalker counts your steps without watching you.

No email. No phone number. No ads. No trackers. Your account is 24 words that only you hold, and your steps, motion and GPS stay on your phone.

WALK FOR FREEDOM
Every step moves you along real journeys of people who moved forward when it cost them. Start with The North Star: Harriet Tubman's corridor from Dorchester County, Maryland to Philadelphia, with postcards that unlock as you walk. More journeys are on the way.

HANDS-FREE
Just walk. VeilWalker notices after about 27 seconds and starts your walk automatically. Stop for a red light and it pauses; stand still a few minutes and it wraps up on its own.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Tor is on by default, so even we can't see your IP address
• Raw motion and GPS data never leave your phone and are deleted after 30 days
• Only signed daily step totals are synced
• No data is ever sold, rented or shared

BADGES, RANKS AND CLANS
• 91 badges, from First Steps to the Marathon and secret ones
• Ranks from Bronze to Platinum frame every badge
• Outlaw ranks from Stowaway to Kingpin, 15 collectible Outlaws on Foot cards and 11 Hidden Peaks
• Pick a permanent handle and an ASCII profile picture
• Join or start a clan: [ROX] HashCat

POINTS THAT ARE YOURS
Collect points for walking. Every point is signed and chained on your own phone so you can verify your balance. Points never expire. Points have no cash value.

HELP PEOPLE REACH TOR
Optionally host a Snowflake proxy while you walk to help people in censored countries reach the open internet, and frost every badge you own.

Built by 3XCypher. Walk the world. Leave no trace.
